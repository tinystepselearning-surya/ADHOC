## Reconciliation and metrics
Media cost = actual net advertising spend; total acquisition cost = media + allocated agency/SEO/creative and other agreed acquisition costs (no duplicate invoice lines; distinguish shared overhead).
CPL = media cost / unique valid attributable leads; CPQL = media cost / unique qualified attributable leads; media cost per admission = media spend / deduplicated attributable confirmed admissions; fully loaded CAC = total acquisition cost / deduplicated attributable confirmed admissions.
Lead-to-admission rate = confirmed admissions from mature enquiry cohort / valid leads in that same cohort. ROAS = net attributable revenue / media spend; specify earned vs collected and observation window. Marketing ROI = (attributable contribution before acquisition cost - total acquisition cost) / total acquisition cost; contribution deducts agreed delivery costs, refunds and concessions without double counting. Attributable does not prove incremental causal impact. For zero denominator or unknown data, report N/A, not zero. Do not add Google and Meta conversion totals without cross-channel deduplication. Report unattributed admissions and match rates; use first-party controlled reconciliation and disclosed attribution rule. Segment brand/nonbrand, organic/paid/referral/direct/unknown, campus/programme, mature cohort and comparable season; avoid premature comparisons of recent leads with mature cohorts.
