## Interpretation
Google Search Central guidance: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet (retrieved 2026-11-27). Google may use descriptions in snippets but often generates snippets from page content; accurate unique descriptions are recommended, especially for critical URLs. Absence alone does not prove ranking penalty, indexing failure, lower CTR or admissions loss. Open Graph descriptions are not substitutes for the standard description tag. Maintenance or obsolete pages require indexability review before adding metadata.

## Reproduction

Inspect original page source for meta name="description"; distinguish it from property="og:description". Compare with the supplied crawl and Yoast configuration.
