# SR-016 — Ambiguous medical entrance preparation terminology
A01/A12. Date 2026-11-27. Proposed priority Medium. Verified public wording; intended meaning pending. Open.
Homepage https://sriraama.in/ section Everything You Need To Succeed lists "Boards + NEET + AIIMS + JIPMER". NTA official 2026 NEET introduction https://neet.nta.nic.in/about-department/introduction/ identifies NEET UG as common medical admission examination; MCC https://mcc.nic.in/about-department/ states it conducts AIIMS and JIPMER UG counselling since 2020–21. AIIMS and JIPMER are institutions, not separate current MBBS entrance examinations. Wording may intend target institutions rather than separate exams, so ambiguity rather than proven false teaching. Ask whether separate entrance preparation is claimed and when academic content was last checked against official notices. Closure: clarify current admissions pathway using approved academic wording and cite official guidance where useful. No site changes.
