# SR-015 — National first-place NEET coaching claim requires substantiation
A01/A12. Date 2026-11-27. Proposed priority High editorial verification. Verified published wording, supporting evidence pending. Open.
Source https://sriraama.in/intermediate-bipc-with-neet-coaching-in-hyderabad/ introductory text compares NEET centres across India and claims it has "consistently stood in the first place for over the years"; homepage https://sriraama.in/ repeats first-place wording in NEET course card. No named ranking body, methodology, specific years or supporting reference identified with claim in retrieved text. Not a finding that the claim is false, or a legal determination. Ask for independently verifiable ranking evidence, dates, scope and approval record; distinguish internal outcomes from comparative national ranking. Closure: substantiate or revise approved wording, attach evidence and review similar comparative claims. No site changes.
