## Interpretation
Google Search Central: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link (checked 2026-11-27). Google recommends distinctive descriptive page titles and may generate different title links for repetitive or unclear titles. The short/long URL labels suggest distinct duration-related intent; actual offerings, demand, content differentiation, rankings, CTR and keyword cannibalisation require verification. Identical titles alone do not prove a ranking penalty or duplicate page content.

## Reproduction
Inspect original HTML title elements for each pair using View Page Source or a fresh read-only curl fetch. Compare title, canonical, content and intended target queries. For Google title links and cannibalisation, use actual SERP observations and GSC query-by-page exports rather than assuming title tags are displayed verbatim.
