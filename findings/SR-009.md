# SR-009 — No dedicated admissions process landing page in observed inventory
Workstream A02/A09/A15. Date 2026-11-27. Priority high opportunity (proposed). Evidence: bounded crawl inventory and competitor public content. Open.

Reopened audit.json: 31 distinct self-canonical URLs, none with an admissions-specific URL or title. Homepage and contact page have enquiry forms (SR-007); this does not mean admissions are unavailable or no admissions information exists elsewhere. Ignite's https://admissions.ignite.academy/ demonstrates a dedicated admissions page and programme comparison; Nine Education's https://nineeducation.in/intermediate-admissions-2027-28-hyderabad/ demonstrates a cycle-specific decision guide. Examples are competitor-authored. No proof of actual rank or conversion advantage.

Ask: Where is the authoritative admissions journey, entry requirements, process, dates, required documents and campus selection documented? Is there a separate campaign landing page not in the crawl? Show approved admissions content and query/page/conversion reports. Closure: verify CMS/campaign inventory, confirm active admission cycle and facts, assess whether a dedicated user-focused destination is justified, then verify navigation and attribution. No website changes.
