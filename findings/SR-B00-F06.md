## Interpretation and limitations
Similar metrics and copied metadata suggest an alternative or copied homepage, but do not prove substantive content equivalence, staging status or accidental publication. Web retrieval of home-copy returned DisabledError on independent recheck 2026-11-27; homepage retrieval succeeded. The original collector retained metrics/hashes, not full HTML bodies. Actual visible content comparison, CMS publication history, Google-selected canonicals, actual indexation, traffic and backlinks remain pending. No ranking or conversion loss proven.

Google guidance: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls ; https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/canonicalization-troubleshooting . Canonical annotations and redirects are strong signals, sitemap inclusion weaker; Google makes the final canonical selection. Duplicate content alone is not a spam violation.

## Review questions
1. What is the business purpose of /home-copy/ and who approved publication?
2. If this is an old/test/duplicate homepage, why is it public, self-canonical, index-permitted and included in the XML sitemap?
3. Can you demonstrate both pages side by side, their WordPress revision history and any active campaigns using home-copy?
4. Show URL Inspection for both pages (user-declared and Google-selected canonical, index status), and query/page performance, backlinks and traffic before deciding disposition.
5. What release process prevents obsolete or test pages from entering the public index?

## Closure
Determine intended purpose and substantive similarity first. If genuinely redundant and no valid standalone purpose, consider approved permanent redirect to the equivalent primary page, removing obsolete URL from sitemap and updating references. If a necessary accessible near-duplicate, assess canonicalisation; if an intentionally distinct public page, differentiate its content and metadata and retain appropriate self-canonical. If staging/private, use access controls rather than relying on robots. Verify source, sitemap and GSC after authorised changes. No website changes made.
