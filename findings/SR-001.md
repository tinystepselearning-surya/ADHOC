## Evidence

Page heading: "Intermediate (MPC) with JEE Main & Advanced".

Section: "Our course and their features".

First bullet: "Regular NEET Coaching".

The page was retrieved through a read-only web fetch on 27 November 2026. The retrieved heading was on line 8 and the section and bullet on lines 34–36 of the extracted text. These are extraction line numbers, not HTML source line numbers. The surrounding copy describes engineering and JEE preparation.

## Reproduction

1. Open the affected URL in a normal browser.
2. Locate the heading "Our course and their features".
3. Inspect the first bullet and compare it to the programme title.
4. Capture a dated browser screenshot showing the programme identity and bullet (pending; no screenshot claimed).

## Expected versus observed

Expected: the feature list accurately describes the MPC–JEE offering, or explicitly explains any genuinely offered cross-programme feature.

Observed: an unexplained NEET coaching feature is listed in the JEE programme's feature list.

## Assessment

The mismatch is verified; whether NEET is actually offered as part of this programme requires academic confirmation. High priority is recommended because this is a core admissions page. Potential impact includes confusing parents, weakening academic credibility and diluting topical relevance. Actual admissions losses or ranking effects have not been measured.

## Review questions

1. Is regular NEET coaching genuinely part of this MPC–JEE programme? If so, why is it not explained?
2. Who approves academic content before publication?
3. When was this specific page last reviewed for accuracy?
4. Can you show the publication/revision history and academic approval process?

## Closure criteria

- Academic owner confirms the intended feature.
- Website team corrects or clarifies the feature as appropriate.
- Re-fetch and browser review confirm the accurate copy.
- Record the team's explanation and review controls without assuming individual responsibility.

## Limitations

Public text evidence only; visual capture, change history, team responsibility and measured commercial impact remain unverified. No form submitted or website changes made.
